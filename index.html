  • March 12, 2026

    Reverse Shells vs. Bind Shells

    Explains the fundamental differences between bind and reverse shells in cybersecurity, using Netcat as the primary example. Bind shells require the attacker to connect to a listening port on the victim; reverse shells force the victim to initiate the connection to the attacker. Reverse shells are preferred for bypassing modern firewall controls.
