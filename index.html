A little about me

I like understanding a system well enough to set it up properly, and then well enough to see exactly how it fails.

Most of my projects start as infrastructure: LDAP single sign-on, mail servers, replicated databases, a full DNS tree. Then I turn around and attack them. The eJPT confirmed the offensive side, with recon, exploitation, privilege escalation and pivoting using Nmap, Metasploit, Burp Suite and plenty of Linux.

I've also completed cybersecurity job simulations with Deloitte, EY, Mastercard and AIG, and I teach coding and online safety to primary school pupils as a volunteer.
